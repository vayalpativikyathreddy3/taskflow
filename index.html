What's inside

Everything a task list turns into once it grows up

🗂️

Kanban board

Drag tasks across custom columns instead of scrolling a flat list to find what's next.

📅

Calendar view

See deadlines laid out by day, so nothing lands on your desk as a surprise.

📊

Analytics

Charts on completion trends, categories, and workload — built on your real task data, not vanity numbers.

⏱️

Pomodoro timer

Tie a focus session to a specific task, and let the break remind itself.

☑️

Subtasks

Break one task into a checklist and watch the progress badge fill in as you go.

⏳

Time tracking

Start, pause, and see exactly how long each task actually took — not how long you guessed.

🤖

AI assistant

Ask it to prioritize, plan your day, or flag risks. Runs free and local — no account, no API key.

☁️

Gmail cloud sync

Sign in with your Gmail account to keep tasks in step across devices, stored privately in your own Drive.
