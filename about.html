About TaskFlow

A task manager that stays out of your way.

TaskFlow started from a simple frustration: every task app worth using eventually wants an account, a subscription, or a dozen tabs open just to plan a day. So we built one that doesn't — a kanban board, calendar, analytics, Pomodoro timer, and a free local AI assistant, all living in a single page you can open and use immediately.

What we believe

A few opinions baked into every feature

01

Your data is yours

Tasks live in your browser by default. Cloud sync is opt-in, uses your own Gmail/Google account, and stores backups in a private Drive folder only TaskFlow can read.

02

No account tax

Open the app and start typing. No sign-up wall, no trial countdown, no feature locked behind a paywall to make a to-do list usable.

03

Smart, not sneaky

The built-in AI assistant runs entirely on your own task data, right in your browser — free, offline-capable, and with nothing ever sent to a third-party server.

04

Simple by construction

Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no framework, no build step, no dependency tree to fight. Anyone can open the source and understand it in an afternoon.

05

One workspace, not ten tools

Kanban, calendar, analytics, Pomodoro, subtasks, and time tracking share one task list — so switching apps stops being part of getting things done.

06

Yours to change

Fork it, restyle it, rip out what you don't need. There's no build pipeline standing between you and the code.
